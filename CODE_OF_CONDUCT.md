# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ThreeToks to be a welcoming project to contribute to and use,
regardless of experience level, background, or how small your first PR is.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, PRs, and discussions.
- Assume good faith; disagree about code and design without making it
  personal.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully — most reviews here are about
  measured tradeoffs (see [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md)), not taste.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, PRs, or discussions.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: the repository (issues, PRs, code
review, commits) and any official communication channels tied to the
project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **mlg.fcu@gmail.com**. Reports will be
reviewed and handled with discretion. Maintainers may remove, edit, or
reject comments, commits, and contributions that don't align with this
Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for
behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor
Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
